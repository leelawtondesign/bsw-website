Key to the success of the project was keeping services running with no down time, achieved by phasing the works programme and controlling individual zones during switch-over.

## Heating

- De-commissioning of the existing gas boilers
- Installation of 4 no. Daikin ASHPs, 44.89kW each, and thermal store
- Upgrade of heating system from a 2-zone to a 4-zone system control
- Trend BMS controls installed and added to the LFB remote central monitoring strategy
- All radiators and associated pipework replaced

## Hot & Cold Water Services

Installation of the following:

- CAT 5 break tank and booster set
- Adveco FPi32 12kW ASHP
- 24kW Adveco electric boiler
- Adveco 470 litre water cylinder with 6kW backup immersion heater
- New hot and cold distribution to all existing outlets
