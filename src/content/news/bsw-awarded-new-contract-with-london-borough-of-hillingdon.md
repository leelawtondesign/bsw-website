BSW Heating Ltd Successfully Awarded London Borough of Islington Communal Heating & Ventilation Contract

BSW secures 10-year term contract starting May 2026 to service and maintain 60 plant rooms and associated dwellings boroughwide.

## The works will include:

- 24/7 Reactive callouts and repairs
- PPM servicing regime for plant rooms and dwellings
- Booster set servicing
- Air conditioning repair and maintenance
- Planned capital projects
- Installation works and upgrades
- Anticipated Value of £20 Million over contract term.
